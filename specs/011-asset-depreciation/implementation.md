# Phase 4 — book depreciation

Approved scope: straight-line monthly schedules, explicit schedule approval, review-before-posting or unattended monthly posting, current book values, month-close warnings, prospective estimate changes, controlled reversals and stopping future charges. Reuse Assets, Records and Accounts closing. Registration remains metadata only. This implements the owner's approved proposal; the earlier handover is supporting context, not independent authorisation.

## Owner walkthrough

1. Open a registered asset. In its Depreciation card, choose **Set up depreciation**. Confirm the start month, useful life (remaining life after opening/manual history), and monthly automation. Choose the depreciation Expense account once; later assets reuse approved defaults. Cost comes from the source. More options contains residual and account overrides. Reuse the established contra mapping for that cost account, or approve creation of a managed separate accumulated-depreciation account. Account creation requires accounts.create; preview never creates accounts.
2. Preview the ordinary charge, final month/book value, account mappings and missed-month count/range/total. Expand the full schedule when needed. Choose **Save setup; review missed months later** (default), or explicitly **Approve and post missed months now**. Saving setup never authorizes automatic posting of pre-approval months. The policy is full monthly straight-line charges, integer cents, with the remaining rounding cents in the last period. RM12,000 over 36 months at zero residual produces 35 × RM333.33 and a final RM333.45. There is no daily proration or business-use multiplier.
3. For legacy assets, first confirm a reconciled month-end opening balance. Start after that opening month and enter remaining life, not historical original life. Existing cost/depreciation ledger balances must reconcile; approving a profile never creates an opening entry.
4. Open **Assets → Depreciation**, select a completed month, review the whole-book batch and exceptions, then **Post depreciation** and confirm. Earlier due months must be handled first. Each successful invocation creates one balanced journal, with one expense/contra pair and posting key per asset. Multiple approvers or later catch-up groups can legitimately produce several runs in one month; their journals are listed separately.
5. Inspect the journal from Records or the asset schedule. Debit Depreciation Expense / credit Accumulated Depreciation. Expense lines save an explicit Non-deductible / 0% tax classification and reason; capital allowances remain separate. Ordinary record edit/delete/void and tax-override paths cannot alter generated journals. The depreciation run is the correction boundary.
6. Automatic mode checks on server startup and hourly while running, using the configured server business timezone (`TZ`, consistent with month closing). It catches up completed, open months covered by future automation authority after downtime. Earlier historical gaps wait for explicit review. The asset’s **Review missed months** action confirms a selected-asset catch-up, posting in chronological order and one transaction. Any blocked month rolls back the entire catch-up; it never posts another asset or shifts dates. Nothing runs while the application is offline. Each run is attributed to its approving user, whose current permissions are checked again; delegated credentials cannot grant unattended authority. Revoked approvals, changed sources, missing accounts, earlier gaps and closed months remain visible rather than posting into another date.
7. Asset detail separates actual posted balances from projected schedule values. The register's net book value and reconciliation follow the selected as-at date. A mid-month as-at excludes month-end journals. Reversals affect balances only from their own posting date.
8. **Manage depreciation → Change remaining schedule** creates another approved profile version, based on remaining NBV, effective after all posted and reversal months. Earlier charges are unchanged. Normal asset metadata saves cannot change approved estimates. If the financial basis changes, review and approve the remaining schedule again. Account transfers and opening-depreciation rewrites after posting require a separate reviewed accounting correction.
9. **Manage depreciation → Stop future depreciation** requires an effective month and reason. This is not a disposal. For a posted mistake, use **Reverse whole run** from the monthly tab, choose a completed open correction month and give a reason. Reverse later active runs for the affected assets first. The original journal remains intact, a linked opposite journal records the correction, and every unposted charge under the affected schedules stops. Reversed asset/month keys remain reserved; resumption requires a new approved profile after the correction month. Closed original periods are not rewritten.
10. Accounts closing warns about due but unposted approved charges, with links to the relevant depreciation month. The warning is informational. Closing still prevents those charges from subsequently posting until the month is explicitly reopened.

## Persistence and write boundaries

- Additive migration `0044_asset_depreciation.sql` adds profiles, runs and per-asset monthly allocations. It changes no existing table or historical row and activates no automation. CHECK columns use unqualified identifiers to avoid the SQLite rename compatibility problem encountered in 0043.
- Approved profile versions retain financial basis, remaining life, residual, accounts, posting mode, approving user and reason. Same-start revisions are permitted while wholly unposted; schedule selection uses the latest approved version at that month. Posted history always retains its original profile.
- A unique `(asset_id, month)` allocation prevents duplicate runs, including after a reversal. Immediate transactions serialize preview revalidation, closing guards, journal/allocations and audit writes. Any failure rolls back the whole invocation; SSE emits happen only after commit.
- Preview hashes cover the current asset/profile/history, reviewed settings and closing boundary. The UI freezes the preview settings and version used by approval/post confirmation. Automatic runs use the same posting operation and transaction boundaries as manual runs.
- Generated original and correction journals have internal ledger provenance, protected service mutations and restrictive run links. They do not use the ordinary same-date void-pair relation: excluding an original/correction pair from history would incorrectly hide expenses in an earlier closed month.
- Profile account references participate in account deletion/merge checks. Historical profile references remain retained. Direct schedule posting requires accounts.read, records.read, accounts.update, records.create and records.adjust; reversal additionally requires records.void.

## Verification and limits

Run isolated migrated database tests using the existing safe preload, plus type checking, targeted lint/formatting, schema-generation consistency and the existing regression manifest. Financial tests cover cents, final residuals, tiny and safe-integer bases, duplicate keys/retries, stale previews, batch atomicity, tax classification, closing, legacy openings, prospective revisions, immutable journals, cross-period/same-period reversal history, stopping, revoked approvals and separate automatic approvers.

Do not start the application against the configured book or run browser UI checks: repository policy leaves visual/interaction verification to the owner. No deployment, live migration or automatic live posting is performed during development.

Disposal/sale accounting, impairment, revaluation, declining balance, daily proration, tax capital allowances and fiscal-year tax preparation remain outside this phase. Manual ledger depreciation stays a reconciliation difference until explicitly linked; it is never silently assigned or posted again.

Approval and further posting pause when the selected accumulated-depreciation account has unexplained manual balances. This avoids charging again over existing manual depreciation. The asset shows the account name, ledger amount, register-explained amount and difference, with record links. Use **Manage depreciation → Link existing manual depreciation** to select an existing journal/account pair, allocate the amount for this asset, and confirm its covered months and reason. Shared journals can be divided across assets up to the original expense/contra capacity. Links do not change the ledger; register balances follow the original journal date. Opening-covered periods, overlaps, over-allocation and periods before in-service are refused. Link history before engine postings; reviewed opening balances remain the route for mixed or more complex historical corrections. Linked journals cannot be edited, deleted or voided until their links are removed. Removal is available before engine postings and stops dependent forecasts; subsequent setup starts after linked periods and journal dates.

Upgrade gives them a future boundary at the current server-local month; older unposted gaps require explicit catch-up, rather than inheriting retroactive authority. Startup migration checks cover this preservation and restart idempotence.

Managed accounts are created in the same approval transaction and roll back if approval/posting fails. Browser/visual verification remains with the owner.
