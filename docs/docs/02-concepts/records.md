# Records

A record is one event with money. Each record tells where the money came from and where it
went. This page tells what a record is and which kinds of record Akaun keeps.

## One list of everything that happened

Akaun keeps all records in one list. The **Records** screen shows each expense, income,
transfer, payment, opening balance, invoice and journal entry. Expenses and income are not in
separate lists. To see one kind only, use the **Kind** filter on the **Records** screen.

Each record names at least two accounts. Money goes out of one account and into a different
account. For example, you pay 50.00 from **Bank** for a software subscription. The money came
out of **Bank** and went into **Software**.

## Record kinds

The kind tells what a record is. The **Records** screen shows the kind of each record in the
**Kind** column. There are seven kinds:

| Kind | What it is | When you see it |
|---|---|---|
| **Expense** | Money that the business spends. | Money goes out of a money account, or out of **Accounts Payable**, into an expense category or into a thing that you buy and keep. |
| **Income** | Money that the business earns. | Money comes from an income category into a money account, or into **Accounts Receivable**. |
| **Transfer** | Money that moves between two of your own accounts. | Money goes from one money account into a different money account, for example from **Bank** to **Cash**. |
| **Payment** | Money that pays an amount that is owed. | Money goes out of a money account to pay what the business owes. Or money from a customer comes into a money account to pay what the customer owes. |
| **Opening balance** | The money in an account on the day that you start to use Akaun. | You give an account a starting balance on its account page. |
| **Invoice** | The amount that a customer owes for an invoice. | You mark an invoice as sent. Akaun makes this record for you. If you cancel the invoice, Akaun removes it. |
| **Journal entry** | A record that does not fit the other kinds. Usually it is a correction. | The accounts on the record do not match an everyday kind, or the record has extra lines that are not an everyday split. |

A money account is an account where money is kept, for example **Cash** or **Bank**. A
category tells what the money was for. See
[Accounts and categories](./accounts-and-categories.md).

## You do not choose the kind

The record form does not ask for the kind. It asks for two accounts: the account
on the **out of** line, and the account on the **into** line. Akaun finds the kind from the two accounts that you choose.

For example, money goes out of **Bank** and into **Software**. **Software** is an expense
category, so the record is an **Expense**. Money goes out of **Bank** and into **Cash**. Both are
money accounts, so the record is a **Transfer**.

If you change an account on a record, Akaun finds the kind again. A **Journal entry** needs the
**Adjustments** permission. See [How a record moves money](./how-an-entry-works.md).

## Each record has its own page

Click a record on the **Records** screen to open its page. The page shows the amount, the status,
the accounts and the details of the record. It also shows the contact, the payments, the
attachments and the **History** of the record.

Each record page has its own web address. Copy the address from the browser to share the record
with a different user. That user must have permission to see records.
